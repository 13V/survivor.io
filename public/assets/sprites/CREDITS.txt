In-game character, enemy and boss sprites are from the "Topdown Shooter" pack
by Kenney (https://kenney.nl), released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

CC0 means these assets are public domain and free for personal and commercial
use. Crediting Kenney is appreciated but not required.

Mapped into the game (baked to match each procedural slot at load time):
  player.png  <- Survivor 1 / survivor1_machine
  enemy0.png  <- Zombie 1   / zoimbie1_stand   (basic)
  enemy1.png  <- Hitman 1   / hitman1_hold     (fast)
  enemy2.png  <- Robot 1    / robot1_stand     (tank)
  boss.png    <- Robot 1    / robot1_machine   (boss)

Projectiles, blades, gems and the ground remain procedurally generated.
If these PNGs are removed, the game falls back to procedural art automatically.

In-game character, enemy and boss sprites are from the "Zombie Apocalypse Tileset"
by Ittai Manero (https://ittaimanero.itch.io/zombie-apocalypse-tileset).

License (from the pack's itch.io page):
  "These assets may be used in both personal and commercial projects, may be edited
   and added to as needed, but may NOT be redistributed or re-sold, with or without
   modifications. Credit is highly appreciated."

IMPORTANT: because redistribution is not permitted, the raw sprites are kept ONLY in
this PRIVATE repository. Do not make this repository public, and do not otherwise
redistribute these PNGs. Crediting Ittai Manero in the shipped game is appreciated.

Mapped into the game (one frame each, baked to match each procedural slot and
rendered with nearest-neighbour scaling so the low-res pixel art stays crisp):
  player.png  <- Player Character Walking (frame 1)
  enemy0.png  <- Skinny Zombie  (basic / kind 0)
  enemy1.png  <- Kid Zombie     (fast  / kind 1)
  enemy2.png  <- Big Zombie     (tank  / kind 2)
  boss.png    <- Big Zombie     (boss, upscaled)

Sprites are drawn upright (no aim-rotation) and mirrored horizontally by movement,
which suits top-down pixel art. Projectiles, blades, gems and the ground remain
procedurally generated. If these PNGs are removed, the game falls back to
procedural art automatically.
